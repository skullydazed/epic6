# Egg Nog Pie

## Summary

Egg nog pie is a seasonal no-bake pie. Preparation takes about 10 minutes, plus waiting. The original recipe estimates about 8 hours from starting to eating and makes one 10-inch (25 cm) pie.

## Components

| Amount | Component |
| --- | --- |
| 2 cups | Egg nog pudding, prepared below. |
| 2 cups | Whipped cream, prepared below. |
| One 10-inch crust | Graham cracker crust; instructions are not included. |

## Equipment

| Quantity | Item |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Small bowl, about 4 oz or 100 mL. |
| 2 | Medium mixing bowls, about 2 quarts or 2 L each. |
| 1 | Large mixing bowl, about 4 quarts or 4 L. |
| 1 | Saucepan. |
| 1 | Hand mixer or whisk. |
| 1 each | Fork, rubber scraper, and ladle. |

## Egg nog pudding

### Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
| --- | --- |
| One envelope, 7 g | Unflavored gelatin. |
| 2 tablespoons, 30 mL | Cold water. |
| 2 cups, approximately 500 mL | Egg nog. |

### Method

1. Put the cold water in the small bowl. Gently sprinkle the gelatin into it, stir gently, and let it sit for 15 minutes. Gently rake the gelatin into small clumps with a fork.
2. Meanwhile, warm 1 cup of the egg nog in the saucepan over low heat. It should be nicely warm, not hot; the purpose is to dissolve the gelatin, so only part of the egg nog needs heating.
3. Add the gelatin and continue heating until it is completely dissolved, with no visible trace remaining. The original recipe estimates 3–5 minutes.
4. Remove from the heat and add the remaining egg nog.
5. Transfer to a mixing bowl and chill until set, about 2 hours in the original recipe.

## Whipped cream

### Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
| --- | --- |
| 1 cup, approximately 250 mL | Heavy whipping cream, not already whipped cream. |
| 1/4 cup, approximately 60 mL | Sugar. |
| 1/2 teaspoon, 2.5 mL | Almond extract. |
| 1 teaspoon, 5 mL | Rum extract. |
| 1/4 teaspoon, approximately 1 mL | Salt. |

### Method

1. Put the heavy whipping cream in the large mixing bowl and whisk briefly. A hand mixer can be used.
2. Add the sugar and continue whisking until the cream is no longer runny; this takes several minutes.
3. Transfer the whipped cream to a medium mixing bowl and chill until the pudding is ready.
4. Just before combining it with the pudding, add the almond extract, rum extract, and salt. Whisk briefly once more until fluffy.

## Assemble the pie

1. Put the set egg nog pudding in the large mixing bowl. Whisk until its texture is even, without lumps or clumps.
2. Gently fold in the whipped cream in small portions. Add a dollop, then use the rubber scraper to fold the pudding over it.
3. Ladle the mixture into the graham cracker crust. It should be thick like pudding, rather than runny like cream.
4. Refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
